Privacy Policy

FlowDesk — WhatsApp Task Operations · Last updated: 23 July 2026

1. Who we are

FlowDesk is a task-management platform that helps organisations assign, track, and complete work, with notifications delivered over WhatsApp. It is operated by CaratSense for its business clients ("your organisation").

2. Information we collect

  • Account data — your name, work email address, role, and preferred language, provided by your organisation's administrator.
  • Phone number — your WhatsApp-enabled mobile number, provided by your organisation so we can send you task notifications.
  • Task data — tasks assigned to you, their status, deadlines, comments, and related activity.
  • WhatsApp messages — replies you send to our WhatsApp business number (e.g. status updates such as "done"), including voice notes, used solely to update your tasks.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you task assignment and reminder notifications on WhatsApp.
  • To update task status based on your replies.
  • To show dashboards and reports to your organisation's managers and administrators.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

Your information is visible only to your organisation and to the service providers that host the platform (cloud hosting and database providers, and Meta Platforms for WhatsApp message delivery), each acting under their own privacy and security terms.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your organisation uses FlowDesk. When your organisation's account ends, or on request, your data is deleted.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as salted hashes, and access to the database is restricted.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, contact your organisation's administrator, or email us at claude@caratsense.in with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete your account data and associated messages within 30 days.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: claude@caratsense.in
